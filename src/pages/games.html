Kiko Dash 🐕

Kiko has escaped with both wedding rings swinging from her necklace. Jump the wedding admin through four scenes — a new one every 250 metres — and keep the rings from the paperwork.

An epic backstory

Kiko, the beloved Uzwyshyn-Jones family dog, has been entrusted with the most important job of Kazimir and Megan's wedding: ring bearer. With both wedding rings safely attached to her collar, she sets off towards the Cathedral. But disaster strikes when she notices something rustling in the bushes. It's a squirrel... a bilka. The squirrel flicks its bushy tail and flashes a mischievous wink. No one knows where the rivalry began. It's certainly older than living memory. But one thing is clear: the feud is real. The rivalry is fierce. And, thanks to their remarkably similar size, the competition is surprisingly evenly matched. The taunting is simply too much for Kiko to ignore. She takes off at full speed, or at least as fast as her little legs can carry her. In an instant she's a blur, racing after her longtime nemesis in an epic chase. Just as victory seems within reach, the squirrel makes its escape, darting out of reach and up a nearby tree. The feud will have to wait for another day. Suddenly, Kiko remembers the wedding rings. The wedding! She looks around in panic… but there's a problem. In the heat of the chase, she has wandered far from her route and no longer recognises where she is. Kiko is lost.

Now the fate of the wedding rests in your hands. Kiko must find her way back to the Cathedral in time to deliver the rings, navigating four exciting worlds filled with wedding-themed distractions and unexpected obstacles. Your mission is to guide her safely to the ceremony before it's too late. Will love triumph, or will the wedding be left ringless? The adventure begins now.

How to play

Space, up, or tap jumps (hold for a higher hop).

Down (arrow) ducks or drops her faster out of a jump.

On a phone, tap Tap to Play for full screen in landscape — Jump and Duck sit over the game.

Spare rings hang in the air: catch them, and five in a row completes the set.

Get a barvinok to provide a temporary shield.
