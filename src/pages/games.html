Kiko Dash 🐕

Kiko has escaped with both wedding rings swinging from her necklace. Jump the wedding admin through four scenes — a new one every 250 metres — and keep the rings from the paperwork.

How to play

Space, up, or tap jumps (hold for a higher hop). Down ducks a tossed bouquet, or drops her faster out of a jump. On a phone, tap Tap to Play for full screen in landscape — Jump and Duck sit over the game.

Spare rings hang in the air: catch them, and three in a row completes the set. A whisker of a miss still counts. A glowing barvinok lets her breeze through the admin for a moment.

3D mode runs the same chase with lights, bloom, and a proper little world. Scores and levels stay shared.
