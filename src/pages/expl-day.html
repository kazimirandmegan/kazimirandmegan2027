Classic day trips
  • Windsor — Historic riverside town centred on the world's oldest occupied castle. Tour Windsor Castle and St George's Chapel, walk through the old town, and cross the bridge to Eton. ~1 hr 30 min · £20–35
  • Stonehenge & Salisbury — Britain's most famous prehistoric monument paired with a magnificent medieval cathedral city. Visit Stonehenge, then Salisbury Cathedral and its surviving copy of Magna Carta. ~2 hr 45 min · £55–85
  • Bath — Elegant Georgian city of Roman baths and Georgian crescents. Visit the Roman Baths and Bath Abbey, admire the Royal Crescent, and cross Pulteney Bridge. ~2 hr 25 min · £45–90
  • Liverpool — The UK's music capital and home of the Beatles. Visit the Albert Dock and Beatles Story, explore the waterfront, and stay for the city's legendary nightlife. ~2 hr 30–40 min · £55–120
Nature & scenery
  • Whitstable — Small Kent seaside town famous for oysters and colourful beach huts. Eat oysters by the harbour, browse Harbour Market, and walk the coast. ~1 hr 55 min · £35–55
  • Seven Sisters & Eastbourne — Dramatic white chalk cliffs above the English Channel. Walk the coastal path, visit Beachy Head, and finish with fish and chips by the sea. ~2 hr 30 min · £40–65
  • The Cotswolds — Rolling countryside of honey-coloured villages, farm shops, and country pubs. Drive through Stow-on-the-Wold or Broadway, with a pub lunch en route. ~1 hr 45 min–2 hr 30 min by car · ~£40–70 per car
  • The Chilterns — Rolling hills, beech woodland, and pretty villages on St Albans' doorstep. Walk from Great Missenden (Roald Dahl country) or drive up to Ivinghoe Beacon for views. 30–50 min · £10–20
  • Rye — A beautifully preserved hilltop town with cobbled streets and old inns. Wander Mermaid Street, browse antique shops, and settle in for a long lunch. ~1 hr 50 min · £35–60
  • Stratford-upon-Avon — Shakespeare's hometown of Tudor buildings and riverside scenery. See the Birthplace, Anne Hathaway's Cottage, and catch an RSC performance if timings allow. ~2 hr 40 min · £40–75
Culture & history
  • Cambridge — Beautiful university city of ancient colleges and riverside punting. Tour a college, see King's College Chapel, punt on the Cam, and visit the Fitzwilliam Museum. ~1 hr 20–40 min · £25–40
  • Oxford — Ancient university city of colleges, libraries, and museums. Visit the Bodleian Library, climb a tower, and explore the Ashmolean. ~1 hr 45–2 hr · £30–50
  • Canterbury — UNESCO World Heritage medieval cathedral city. Visit Canterbury Cathedral, explore the cobbled centre, and walk beside the River Stour. ~1 hr 35 min · £35–55
  • Winchester — Handsome cathedral city and former capital of Anglo-Saxon England. Visit Winchester Cathedral, see the Great Hall's Round Table, and walk through the water meadows. ~1 hr 55 min · £30–55
  • York — Remarkably preserved city with Roman, Viking, and medieval history. Visit York Minster, walk the city walls, and explore the Shambles. ~2 hr 25–40 min · £50–120
  • Warwick Castle — Large medieval castle with live historical displays, jousting, and falconry. ~2 hr 15–30 min · £35–70
  • Hampton Court Palace — Henry VIII's Tudor palace on the Thames. Explore the kitchens, get lost in the famous maze, and wander the gardens. ~1 hr 30 min · £15–25
  • Harry Potter Studio Tour — Original sets and filmmaking secrets. Book well ahead — it sells out. ~15–25 min by car · taxi ~£30–70 return; admission extra
  • Brighton — Lively seaside city with the Royal Pavilion, independent shops, and a famous pier. The Thameslink runs direct from St Albans. ~1 hr 40 min · £25–45
