The engagement, and the making of a three-nation wedding

We got engaged on a trip to Cape Cod, and afterward we travelled down to North Carolina to celebrate with family. But little did we know they had been planning the most elaborate, beautiful surprise for us — they organised Zoryana to fly in from Texas and created a surprise engagement party complete with a wedding-themed Jeopardy game, themed cocktails, home cooked meals and desserts, a scripted plan to deceive us and handpainted decorations. Also included are some pictures of when Megan surprised Kazimir with a proposal in the English countryside with a gold engagement chain!

Together with our families we've been keen to use this event as a way to highlight the places and people that have shaped us. We've made liters of rhubarb gin and elderflower cordial from the family garden and flowers across St Albans, we've been drying rose petals from our garden to use as confetti, Zoryana has been helping us every step of the way in designing stationary and we've spent lots of time together dreaming about the future (shoutout to Megan's family for coming to England to plan!)
