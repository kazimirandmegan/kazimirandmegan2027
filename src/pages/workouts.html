Tue 25 · 7:30 PM · Until the cocks crow Led by: Everyone, apparently

Hand to Mouth

The most demanding workout of the week and arguably the most important. St Albans is home to some of England's finest (and oldest) pubs, and it would be rude not to acquaint ourselves with them. The route begins at Ye Olde Fighting Cocks — one of the oldest pubs in England, right next to the Cathedral — and continues at a pace determined entirely by thirst and collective stamina. Cardiovascular benefits: disputed. Community benefits: guaranteed. Proper footwear recommended.

Disclaimer: this is the one workout where we encourage you not to go too hard too early. Pace yourself. This is a marathon, not a sprint.
