The Wedding Week

One week, four nations, and one very tired pair of newlyweds — get excited for the wedding week!

No matter who you are — family or friends — you are welcome to take part in everything you see on this page. The more we get to see you, the better!

Timings will be firmed up nearer the day, so please monitor this website. During the week, we'll have a wedding WhatsApp group for live updates — timings, meeting points, any changes. Even if you're only joining us for part of the week, we'd really recommend being in the group. Free-time ideas are in the Explore section.

Planning your travel? If you can, arriving by Monday or Tuesday and leaving Monday gives you the full week with us. Wednesday to Sunday is the bare minimum for the full wedding experience.

For our American guests — please download WhatsApp before you travel. It uses WiFi/data and won't rack up roaming charges. It's the easiest way to stay in the loop during the week, and is used by essentially everyone in the UK.
