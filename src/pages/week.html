The Wedding Week

One week, four nations, and one very tired pair of newlyweds — get excited for the wedding week!

No matter who you are — family or friends — you are welcome to take part in everything you see on this page. The more we get to see you, the better!

Timings will be firmed up nearer the day, so please monitor this website (which should also be mobile-friendly). During the week, we will have a wedding WhatsApp group chat which will send updates on where the core group is and what we're up to. Even if you don't plan on joining us for much, we'd recommend joining in case there are any issues/delays to events and we need to reach you.

We've included some free-time ideas under Explore; for early birds who don't want to skip working out the week-of, see the Wedding Workouts we've planned (can you tell we are Type A?).

A note for the Americans — we would highly recommend downloading and using WhatsApp during your time here, as you can text with your phone number without racking up any charges (it uses WiFi/data). Plus, this will be the easiest way to communicate with us and to be included in all the wedding-related communications during the week!

Tue25

St Albans Day

Choose your own adventure during the day; music and drinks together at night.
AMFor our Harry Potter nerds (you know who you are), this is a good day to visit Harry Potter World, which is a 30-minute drive from St Albans. You must book this far in advance because tickets are hard to come by. We recommend allowing half a day, though you could spend endless hours there if you really wanted to.
AMThis could also be a good day for out-of-town folks to explore our venue, Hatfield House. The grounds will be open and should (hopefully!!) be in bloom. We haven't been to the actual house yet, but it is supposed to be amazing.
5:00 PMEvensong at the Cathedral — there is a tradition in English cathedrals of Evensong, which is essentially an open 45-minute service of choral music. It is always beautiful. We'll be going together to the St Albans Cathedral for Evensong at 5pm.
EveningSt Albans Pub Crawl, planned by Kazimir's hometown friends. Route TBC, but we will start at Ye Olde Fighting Cocks, one of the oldest pubs in England and right next to the Cathedral. Pints will be consumed, but regardless this is a strictly no-cock-fighting event.
