Travelling to St Albans/England

Which airport should I fly into?

London Luton (LTN) is the closest airport, around 20 minutes from St Albans. Heathrow (LHR) offers the most transatlantic routes (around 45–60 minutes away). Gatwick (LGW) has a direct train connection. Avoid Stansted (STN) if possible, as onward travel takes longer. Don't fly into Southend Airport (SEN) — despite the name, it's not actually in London. If prices are similar, choose Luton for European flights and Heathrow for transatlantic flights.

How do I get to and from London?

See the Explore London page. Trains run frequently between St Albans City and London Kings Cross St Pancras, taking around 20 minutes. You can tap in and out using a contactless card or phone. A return ticket from the station machine is usually slightly cheaper. Tickets can be purchased on the day at no extra cost.

How do I get around St Albans?

St Albans is a small city and can be crossed on foot in under 30 minutes. Uber and Bolt operate locally, with service available throughout the night. There are also always taxis available at the train station.

What should I pack for a trip to the UK?

See the For Americans page. Late May is usually mild to warm. Layering is key, as conditions can change throughout the day. Casual clothing and a lightweight waterproof jacket will cover most situations.

I want to explore St Albans or London. Do you have any recommendations?

Please see the Explore St Albans and Explore London pages — we have loads of recommendations!

I want to explore England or Europe. Do you have any recommendations?

Please see the Explore England and Explore Europe pages. For guests travelling from overseas and extending their trip, we recommend using the week beginning 31 May to explore England or Europe if possible — that way if you're around in the days before the wedding, you have the option to participate in various group activities. These are optional though, so no pressure either way.

What are the details for Vinkopletyny (Thursday 27th)?

Please see the Vinkopletyny page for full details. Key information:

  • Timings: We will meet at Kazimir's family home (28 Beaconsfield Road, St Albans, AL1 3RB; 5 minutes from St Albans Station) to process to St Saviour's Church (a 12-minute walk away) at 18:00. If you cannot join the procession, or if the weather is poor, please meet at St Saviour's Church (AL1 4DF) at 18:30. Taxis from St Michael's Manor take around 5 minutes and cost approximately £5.
  • What to wear: Traditional or national dress from your own culture is warmly welcome; alternatively we welcome Ukrainian clothing in support of Ukraine. Otherwise, please wear whatever is smart, comfortable and easy to dance in. See the Vinkopletyny page for more details.

What are the details for the Ceremony & Reception (Saturday 29th)?

Please see the Ceremony page for full details. Key information:

  • Timings: The ceremony begins at 13:30. Please arrive by 13:00 so all guests can be seated by 13:15.
  • Getting to the ceremony: St Albans Cathedral is a 20-minute walk from St Albans City Station or a 5-minute taxi ride. Parking at the Cathedral is limited.

How are we getting to the reception at Hatfield House?

Transport is provided. We will be travelling on classic London red buses for the approximately 30-minute journey from St Albans Cathedral to Hatfield House.

How do I get home after the reception?

Shuttle buses will run between Hatfield House and St Albans from 20:00 onwards. If you are going to London, it is a 5-minute walk from Hatfield Train Station (20 minutes to Kings Cross Station). Alternatively, taxis, Uber, and Bolt are available to St Albans, costing approximately £15–20 and taking around 15 minutes.

Are children welcome?

Children are very welcome, provided they are named on the invitation. St Saviour's Church, St Albans Cathedral, and Hatfield House all have spaces where children can rest and play. Babysitting options will be available for Vinkopletyny and the reception. At both venues, there are quiet rooms available if helpful. Please keep in mind that there will be loud music, alcohol, and a late finish — though that's all part of a good wedding and we want parents to have a good time!
