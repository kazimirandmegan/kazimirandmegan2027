Choose your adventure

Iconic first-timers
  • Paris — The classic grand European capital of art, boulevards, and landmark architecture. Book the Louvre in advance; the Marais and Montmartre need nothing but appetite and comfortable shoes. Eurostar direct · ~2 hr 20 min station-to-station · £80–220
  • Amsterdam — A compact canal city mixing Golden Age streets, major museums, and lively neighbourhoods. Visit the Rijksmuseum and Van Gogh Museum (book weeks ahead), hire bikes, and eat stroopwafels by the water. Eurostar direct or flight · ~4 hr train / ~1 hr 15 min flight · £90–240
  • Rome — An open-air history book of ancient ruins, Baroque squares, and irresistible trattorias. The Colosseum, Vatican, and Trevi Fountain are unmissable — book timed entry for the first two well ahead. Flight · ~2 hr 30 min airborne · £90–260
  • Barcelona — Mediterranean city of Gaudí architecture, beaches, markets, and late-night dining. Sagrada Família and Park Güell need advance tickets; La Boqueria and the Gothic Quarter do not. Flight · ~2 hr 10 min airborne · £70–230
Culture treasure hunt
  • Florence — A walkable Renaissance masterpiece of galleries, palaces, and Tuscan flavours. The Uffizi and Accademia (David) both require advance booking; the Ponte Vecchio and Oltrarno neighbourhood are free. Flight · ~2 hr 15 min airborne · £100–280
  • Vienna — Imperial palaces, coffee-house culture, classical music, and elegant streets. Tour the Hofburg or Schönbrunn, catch a concert, and linger over a Melange and Sachertorte. Flight · ~2 hr 15 min airborne · £80–240
  • Berlin — A creative capital where 20th-century history meets contemporary art and nightlife. Walk the East Side Gallery, visit the Holocaust Memorial and Checkpoint Charlie, then explore Prenzlauer Berg. Flight · ~1 hr 50 min airborne · £70–220
  • Kraków — A beautiful medieval core with royal history, atmospheric cellar bars, and excellent value. Visit Wawel Castle, walk the Old Town, and take the sobering day trip to Auschwitz-Birkenau. Flight · ~2 hr 25 min airborne · £70–210
Eat your way around
  • Bologna — Italy's porticoed food capital, with medieval towers and superb rail connections for day trips. Eat tagliatelle al ragù at its birthplace, browse Mercato di Mezzo, and climb the Due Torri. Flight · ~2 hr 10 min airborne · £80–230
  • Lyon — France's gastronomic powerhouse combining Roman history, two rivers, and traditional bouchon dining. Try quenelles and andouillette, visit the Traboules passages, and catch the view from Fourvière hill. Eurostar to Paris plus TGV, or flight · ~5–6 hr rail / ~1 hr 40 min airborne · £100–260
  • San Sebastián — A glamorous Basque seaside city famed for pintxos bars and more Michelin stars per capita than almost anywhere. Graze the Old Town bars at night, swim at La Concha beach by day. Flight to Bilbao plus coach, or seasonal direct · usually 6–8 hr total · £120–300
  • Porto — A colourful riverside city of tiled churches, steep lanes, and port-wine lodges across the Douro. Tour a lodge in Vila Nova de Gaia, take the tram to Foz, and eat a francesinha. Flight · ~2 hr 20 min airborne · £80–240
Storybook streets
  • Bruges — A remarkably preserved canal city of Gothic squares, cobblestones, and chocolate shops. Climb the Belfry, drift along the canals by boat, and resist absolutely nothing in the chocolate shops. Eurostar to Brussels plus Belgian train · ~3–3.5 hr rail total · £90–210
  • Prague — A dramatic city of spires, castle courtyards, medieval bridges, and excellent beer. Cross Charles Bridge at dawn before the crowds arrive, tour Prague Castle, and explore Malá Strana. Flight · ~2 hr airborne · £70–220
  • Tallinn — A compact Hanseatic old town paired with Nordic design and forward-thinking digital culture. Walk the medieval walls, explore Toompea Hill, and venture into the creative Telliskivi district. Flight · ~2 hr 45 min airborne · £100–260
  • Dubrovnik — A walled Adriatic city with limestone lanes, island views, and cinematic fortifications. Walk the city walls at sunrise, take the cable car up Srđ Hill, and island-hop to Lokrum. Seasonal flight · ~2 hr 45 min airborne · £120–320
Sun-seeker reset
  • Lisbon — A hilly, light-filled capital with tiled façades, hilltop viewpoints, and Atlantic flavours. Ride tram 28, visit São Jorge Castle, eat pastel de nata in Belém, and explore the riverside LX Factory. Flight · ~2 hr 45 min airborne · £80–240
  • Seville — Andalusian grandeur with orange-scented courtyards, flamenco, and tapas after dark. Visit the Alcázar and Cathedral, catch a flamenco show, and wander the Barrio Santa Cruz. Flight · ~2 hr 45 min airborne · £90–250
  • Nice & the Riviera — A polished Mediterranean base for beaches, markets, and easy coastal train trips. Browse the Cours Saleya market, swim at Villefranche, and take the train west to Monaco or east to Menton. Flight · ~2 hr airborne · £90–270
  • Valletta — A honey-coloured harbour capital with fortifications, sea views, and layered Mediterranean history. Visit St John's Co-Cathedral and the Upper Barrakka Gardens; day-trip to the Three Cities or Mdina. Flight · ~3 hr 15 min airborne · £100–280
Big-nature weekend
  • Reykjavík & the Golden Circle — A small Nordic capital that unlocks geothermal landscapes, waterfalls, and volcanic scenery. Do the Golden Circle (Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss) as a day trip, and soak in the Blue Lagoon or Sky Lagoon. Flight · ~3 hr airborne · £130–350
  • Bergen & the fjords — A colourful harbour city framed by mountains and gateways to spectacular fjords. Take the Flåm Railway into Nærøyfjord, hike up Fløyen, and eat fish at Bryggen wharf. Flight · ~2 hr airborne · £110–300
  • Geneva & Lake Léman — An international lakeside city suited to mountain day trips, elegant lakeside towns, and vineyard excursions. Take the boat to Lausanne or Montreux, visit the Lavaux vineyards, and eye the Alps from the Jet d'Eau. Flight · ~1 hr 40 min airborne · £90–250
  • Madeira — A subtropical Atlantic island of levada walks, mountain ridges, lush gardens, and dramatic coastline. Walk a levada through the laurisilva forest, take the Monte toboggan ride, and explore the market in Funchal. Flight · ~3 hr 50 min airborne · £140–360
After-dark energy
  • Budapest — A grand Danube city combining thermal baths, ruin bars, and striking architecture. Soak in Széchenyi or Rudas baths, explore the Jewish Quarter ruin bars, and walk across Chain Bridge at night. Flight · ~2 hr 30 min airborne · £70–220
  • Madrid — A confident capital built around world-class art, late dinners, and energetic plazas. Visit the Prado and Reina Sofía (Guernica), eat dinner at 10 pm, and bar-hop around La Latina or Malasaña. Flight · ~2 hr 25 min airborne · £70–230
  • Copenhagen — A stylish, bike-friendly harbour city with design, bakeries, and inventive dining. Visit Nyhavn and Tivoli, eat at a new Nordic restaurant, and cycle everywhere — hire a bike from the hotel. Flight · ~1 hr 50 min airborne · £80–240
  • Dublin — A sociable, literary capital with Georgian streets, live music, and easy coastal escapes. Visit the Book of Kells, drink at Mulligan's, explore the DART coastal villages, and catch live music in Temple Bar. Flight or rail-ferry · ~1 hr 20 min airborne · £60–200
No-fly adventure
  • Brussels & Ghent — A two-city Belgian sampler of grand squares, Art Nouveau, medieval canals, and serious beer culture. In Brussels: Grand Place, Magritte Museum, moules-frites. In Ghent: the Gravensteen castle, Graslei waterfront, and Ghent altarpiece. Eurostar direct to Brussels plus local train to Ghent · ~2 hr 30 min total · £80–200
  • Lille — France's best-kept day-trip secret: a lively Flemish-influenced city with handsome squares, serious art, and legendary bakeries. Explore the Vieux-Lille quarter, visit the Palais des Beaux-Arts, and eat a gaufre. Eurostar direct · ~1 hr 20–25 min station-to-station · £60–170
  • Cologne — A Rhine city anchored by a soaring Gothic cathedral, excellent museums, and convivial Kölsch beer halls. Climb the cathedral towers, visit the Chocolate Museum, and drink Kölsch standing at a Brauhaus. Eurostar to Brussels plus high-speed train · ~4–5 hr rail total · £100–240

Practicalities

  • Book Eurostar 2–3 months out for the best fares; check entry requirements for your passport nationality
  • Left-luggage at St Pancras lets you day-trip or weekend-trip straight off your suitcase
  • Cards work everywhere on the continent — no currency wads required
  • Budget airlines (Ryanair, easyJet, Vueling, Wizz) fly from Luton, Stansted, and Gatwick — all reachable from St Albans
