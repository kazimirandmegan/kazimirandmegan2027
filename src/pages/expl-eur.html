Choose your adventure

First-timers
  • Paris — The classic grand European capital of art, boulevards, and landmark architecture. Book the Louvre in advance; the Marais and Montmartre need nothing but appetite and comfortable shoes. Eurostar direct · ~2 hr 20 min · £80–220
  • Amsterdam — A compact canal city mixing Golden Age streets, major museums, and lively neighbourhoods. Visit the Rijksmuseum and Van Gogh Museum (book weeks ahead), hire bikes, and eat stroopwafels by the water. Eurostar or flight · ~4 hr train / ~1 hr 15 min flight · £90–240
  • Rome — An open-air history book of ancient ruins, Baroque squares, and irresistible trattorias. Book the Colosseum and Vatican timed entry well ahead. Flight · ~2 hr 30 min airborne · £90–260
  • Barcelona — Mediterranean city of Gaudí architecture, beaches, markets, and late-night dining. Sagrada Família needs advance tickets; the Gothic Quarter does not. Flight · ~2 hr 10 min airborne · £70–230
Second-timers & city-hoppers
  • Budapest — Two cities on the Danube, with grand thermal baths, ruin bars, and some of Europe's finest architecture. Visit the Széchenyi Baths, cross the Chain Bridge, and eat goulash in the Jewish Quarter. Flight · ~2 hr 30 min airborne · £70–200
  • Prague — One of Europe's most beautiful old towns, with a medieval astronomical clock, castle above the river, and legendary beer halls. Stay in the centre and walk everywhere. Flight · ~2 hr airborne · £60–180
  • Vienna — Imperial grandeur, world-class museums, Schnitzel, and the birthplace of the coffee house. Visit the Kunsthistorisches Museum, the Belvedere, and walk the Ringstrasse. Flight · ~2 hr 15 min airborne · £70–200
  • Berlin — A city that reinvented itself: cold-war history, world-class contemporary art, and the best club scene in Europe. East Side Gallery, Museum Island, and endless currywurst. Flight · ~1 hr 50 min airborne · £55–170
  • Istanbul — The city where Europe meets Asia: mosques, bazaars, the Bosphorus, and extraordinary food. Visit the Hagia Sophia, the Grand Bazaar, and take a Bosphorus ferry. Flight · ~3 hr 30 min airborne · £80–250
  • Athens & the Greek islands — The cradle of Western civilisation, plus island-hopping by ferry. Athens for history (the Acropolis, the Agora), a Greek island for sun and swimming. Flight · ~3 hr 30 min airborne · £80–260
Nature & scenery
  • Reykjavik & Iceland — Otherworldly landscapes of volcanoes, geysers, and the midnight sun in June. Golden Circle day trip, whale watching, and geothermal pools. Flight · ~2 hr 30 min airborne · £100–350
  • Bergen & Norwegian fjords — Europe's fjord gateway. Sail the Nærøyfjord or Geirangerfjord, walk up Mount Fløyen, and eat fresh seafood at the fish market. Flight · ~1 hr 55 min airborne · £80–250
  • Madeira — A volcanic Portuguese island of dramatic cliffs, botanical gardens, and levada walks. Take the historic wicker toboggan down from Monte. Flight · ~3 hr airborne · £80–250
  • Switzerland (Geneva or Zürich base) — Breathtaking mountain scenery accessible by rail. Take the Glacier Express or Bernina Express, visit Interlaken, and swim in alpine lakes. Flight to Geneva or Zürich · ~1 hr 40 min airborne · £80–250
Food & drink
  • Copenhagen — The home of New Nordic cuisine. From Noma's legacy restaurants to street food at Reffen and smørrebrød in old-town lunch spots, this is the food capital of northern Europe. Flight · ~1 hr 50 min airborne · £70–220
  • San Sebastián, Spain — The pintxos capital of the world, with more Michelin stars per capita than anywhere on earth. Eat your way through the Old Town's bars in the evening. Flight to Bilbao · ~1 hr 45 min airborne · £70–220
  • Lisbon — Pastéis de nata, grilled fish, and great wine in a city of hills, trams, and blue-tiled azulejos. Visit Belém, take a tram up to the Alfama, and eat a late dinner like a local. Flight · ~2 hr 20 min airborne · £60–180
  • Naples & the Amalfi Coast — The home of pizza, paired with one of the world's most dramatic coastlines. Take a boat along the Amalfi Coast, visit Pompeii, and eat the best pizza you'll ever have. Flight to Naples · ~2 hr 45 min airborne · £80–230
Culture & history
  • Edinburgh — Scotland's dramatic capital of castle rock, Old Town closes, and world-class festivals. Visit the Castle and Palace of Holyroodhouse, walk the Royal Mile, and climb Arthur's Seat. Flight or train · ~1 hr 20 min flight / ~4–5 hr train · £60–200
  • Normandy, France — The D-Day beaches and the Bayeux Tapestry, within a Eurostar-and-drive of London. Hire a car from Caen and spend a day on the beaches and memorials. Eurostar to Paris, train to Caen · ~4 hr · £90–200
  • Florence — The Renaissance in one city: the Uffizi, Michelangelo's David, the Duomo, and the most beautiful piazzas in Italy. Book the big museums in advance. Flight · ~2 hr 20 min airborne · £80–220
  • Venice — One of the world's great unrepeatable cities — canals instead of roads, nobody drives, and St Mark's Basilica at night. Go in June before the summer crowds peak. Flight · ~2 hr airborne · £75–210
  • Dubrovnik & the Dalmatian Coast — The Pearl of the Adriatic: ancient city walls, crystal-clear sea, and Game of Thrones filming locations. Walk the walls, island-hop by ferry. Flight · ~2 hr 30 min airborne · £80–230

Practicalities

  • Book Eurostar 2–3 months out for the best fares; check entry requirements for your passport nationality
  • Cards work everywhere on the continent — no currency wads required
  • Budget airlines (Ryanair, easyJet, Vueling, Wizz) fly from Luton, Stansted, and Gatwick — all reachable from St Albans
