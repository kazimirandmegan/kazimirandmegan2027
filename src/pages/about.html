Our story

Dear reader,

As we sat down to write this, we found it difficult to write out our love story without sounding endlessly cringe and trite. It turns out that the moments of meeting each other and realising we found the loves of our lives are, unsurprisingly, hard to put into words in a way that feels true instead of kitsch.

Nevertheless we have been told that it is very important to include our story in this wedding website, as this is the content that inquiring minds want to see. We have done our best to summarise the best bits below in a way that hopefully doesn't feel artificial and weird. But we'd love to give you all the juicy details in person when we see you — maybe over a glass of wine?

March 2022 — Set scene: Megan is spending a month in London for the work-placement part of her Master's degree. Her roommates back in Cambridge decide to come down for the weekend to visit, and they bring along some friends from the swim team (cue Kazimir!). Megan throws a party at her flat, which Kazimir comes to. They meet briefly … nothing happens. Her roommate needs to study so she kicks him and his friends out until they meet up again at the club (Kazimir notes that he was made to drink in a random park to pass the time). They dance to a song from Mamma Mia but it feels awkward…nothing happens.

April 2022 — Since the party, word has come down the grapevine that Kazimir thinks Megan is pretty, and this is considered a big deal because Kazimir is usually quiet about these things. Megan is intrigued. In her last weekend in London, Megan is preparing to go to bed (pajamas are ON) when she hears that Kazimir is in London at another party. She rallies (ladies we know this is no small feat) and goes to the party to see who her admirer is. While everyone is drinking in the club, they sit and talk for a while. There is widespread group pressure on Kazimir to make a move, but he prioritises helping his friend get home safely…nothing happens, green flag.

May 2022 — Megan is back in Cambridge, but hasn't heard from Kazimir. She finds this puzzling, given there was an obvious vibe. Weeks pass and curiosity becomes anxiety. She decides it isn't meant to be. Until she realises that she, in fact, also has a phone and the ability to send a message. Why is she waiting on a man to make the first move? She thinks — and until her dying day she will swear on her life that this thought actually did pass through her mind — that she has nothing to fear by reaching out first: Either he ignores her and life goes on, or best case scenario they get married. You're here because she sent that text.

Later on in May 2022, because Kazimir was focused on his exams and not cute girls — Kazimir responded but put Megan on hold for several weeks so he could focus on his exams. Annoying, but green flag.

Later on again in May 2022 — Kazimir finally finishes his exams and Megan is relieved that he remembers who she is. They go on their first date at the Pickerel, a cute riverside pub, followed by ice cream and a cycle home…first kiss!

June 2022 — Many wholesome dates. Floating down the River Cam, homecooked meals, long walks.

July 2022 — One day Kazimir and Megan go on a long walk to a pub on the outskirts of Cambridge. They're the only ones in the pub garden, and they talk at length about the big stuff — their families, their values, their hopes for the future. Something shifts. They both start to picture, separately, a future together. Later that month, Megan finalises her move to London. Kazimir takes her out to dinner and, after hearing this news, asks her to be his girlfriend.

And the rest is history.
