Families & groups

Airbnbs and self-catering cottages around St Albans can work really well! Book early: it's a bank holiday weekend and properties within 30 minutes' walk of the Cathedral quarter go fast.

Staying in London

St Albans is 20 minutes from London St Pancras on the Thameslink, so staying in London is a perfectly viable option — especially if you want to spend a few days there before or after the wedding. We'd recommend somewhere between King's Cross and Covent Garden for easy access to the train and a great base for exploring the city. See the London Explore page for more.

One thing to keep in mind: on the wedding day you'll need to travel in for the ceremony and then out to Hatfield — budget an extra 45 minutes each way.

Getting around
